How to edit

Click a name in a roster to bold it
Pick players chooses the 4 to 10 headshots per team
Drag a headshot onto another to swap them
✕ on a headshot or coach takes them off
Swap on a coach picks another staff member
Click a name, number, position or title to edit it
Double-click a photo to replace it, or drop an image on it
Option + scroll zooms a photo, Option + drag moves it
